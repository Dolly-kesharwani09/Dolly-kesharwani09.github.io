# Portfolio Admin Guide

A simple guide to manage your portfolio from your phone or computer.

---

## 1. How to Open the Admin Panel

1. Open your web browser (Safari or Chrome) on your phone.
2. Go to your portfolio website link and add `/admin/` at the end:
   `https://yourwebsite.com/admin/`
3. Bookmark this page on your phone for quick access.

---

## 2. How to Log In

1. Tap the **Sign in with GitHub** button.
2. Enter your GitHub username and password.
3. If asked, tap **Authorize** to allow access.
4. You will now see your admin dashboard.

---

## 3. How to Add a New Work

1. In the admin menu, tap **Works**.
2. Tap the **Add Work** button (or **+** icon).
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Project Title**: Enter the project name (e.g. *Sankalp Hospital*).
   - **Client Name**: Enter the client or brand name.
   - **Deliverable Type**: Brief description (e.g. *Bi-fold brochure · Print*).
   - **Category**: Select **Branding**, **Print**, or **Social**.
   - **Cover Photo**: Tap **Choose an image** and upload the main photo from your phone.
   - **Extra Pages (Optional)**: If this design has multiple pages (like a calendar or 2-sided card), tap **Add** to upload additional photos.
   - **Featured on Homepage**: Switch this ON only if you want this work highlighted in the main Featured Project section.
   - **Display Order**: Enter a number (e.g. `1` shows first, `2` shows second).
4. Tap the **Save** button in the top right corner.
5. Your new work will appear on your website automatically within 1–2 minutes!

---

## 4. How to Edit an Existing Work

1. Go to **Works**.
2. Tap on the work you want to update.
3. Change the text, replace the photo, or update the category.
4. Tap **Save**.

---

## 5. How to Reorder Works

1. Open the work you want to move.
2. Change the **Display Order** number:
   - Smaller numbers (like `1`, `2`, `3`) appear at the top.
   - Larger numbers appear further down.
3. Tap **Save**.

---

## 6. How to Delete a Work

1. Go to **Works**.
2. Tap the work you want to remove.
3. Tap the trash can icon (or **Delete item** button).
4. Tap **Confirm** to remove it.
5. Tap **Save**.

---

## 7. Recommended Photo Size & Format

Before uploading photos from your phone or laptop:

- **Format**: **JPG** or **WebP** (PNG is okay, but JPG is smaller).
- **File Size**: Keep each photo **under 1 MB** (ideal size is 200 KB to 600 KB).
- **Width**: About **1600 pixels wide**.
- **Tip**: High-resolution photos straight from camera can be 10 MB or more. Compress them first using a free tool like [TinyPNG](https://tinypng.com) or your phone's photo editor before uploading. This keeps your website fast!
